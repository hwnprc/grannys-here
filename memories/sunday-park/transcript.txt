Oh, we went every Sunday. Every single Sunday after church I'd take her down to
the lake in the park, the one with the little bridge and the boats you can rent.
She was maybe six, seven. She always wanted to feed the ducks, and I always told
her not to get too close to the edge, and she never listened, not once. We'd walk
all the way around and she'd be asleep on my shoulder before we got to the car.
The leaves would be down by October and the whole path went gold.
